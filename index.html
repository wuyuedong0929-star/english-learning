3. 10个核心句

1. Please confirm the driver’s full name and phone number.
请确认司机全名和电话号码。
重音/连读提示：confirm / full name / phone number

2. The license plate on the truck does not match the dispatch list.
车上的车牌号与调度清单不一致。
重音/连读提示：license plate / does not match / dispatch list

3. Do not start loading until we finish the document check.
文件核对完成前不要开始装货。
重音/连读提示：Do not / until / document check

4. Please take a clear photo of the empty truck before loading.
装货前请拍一张空车清晰照片。
重音/连读提示：clear photo / empty truck / before loading

5. Record the seal number immediately after the doors are closed.
关门后立即记录封签号。
重音/连读提示：record / immediately / seal number

6. The truck is scheduled to leave at 3:30 p.m.
车辆计划下午3点30分出发。
重音/连读提示：scheduled to / three thirty p.m.

7. If the weight changes, stop the truck and call me first.
如果重量发生变化，先停车并给我打电话。
重音/连读提示：If / stop / call me first

8. The driver must not change the route without approval.
未经批准司机不得改变路线。
重音/连读提示：must not / change the route / approval

9. Send me the signed handover sheet before departure.
出发前把签字交接单发给我。
重音/连读提示：signed / handover sheet / before departure

10. Once everything is verified, dispatch can release the vehicle.
全部核实后，调度可以放行车辆。
重音/连读提示：Once / verified / release the vehicle

7. 真实业务场景

你负责一车钽铌矿装货。调度表显示车牌 RAB 218K，但现场车辆为 RAB 281K。不要直接让车走。先暂停装货，核对是否为录入错误、临时换车，要求调度书面确认，并重新记录司机证件、车辆照片、空车/重车重量、封签号。

9. 15分钟角色扮演

Teacher: The plate number is different from the dispatch list. What will you do?

Student: I will stop loading, verify the driver and vehicle, and ask dispatch for written confirmation before we continue.

Teacher: The driver says he is in a hurry.

Student: I understand, but we cannot release the truck until the documents and vehicle information match.

Teacher: Good. What evidence do you keep?

Student: Driver ID, truck photos, weight records, seal number, handover sheet and the written dispatch confirmation.

参考答题逻辑

Suggested logic: confirm facts, identify the impact, give a clear action, and keep a written record. Do not make a legal or payment conclusion before the evidence and contract support it.
